The first ten nights of Muharram are the high point of the institute's year.

Muhammad Kazim Behjati, head of the institute, speaks from the minbar every night.

Every night is livestreamed on YouTube. Watch on YouTube →

The year at the institute

Six programs, in the order they fall in the Islamic calendar. Dates follow the moon, so ask on WhatsApp for this year's dates.

  1. Muharram

    The first ten nights. Livestreamed on YouTube.

  2. Ramadan

    The month of fasting.

  3. Shab-e-Qadr

    The Nights of Qadr in Ramadan.

  4. Eid prayers

    Prayers on the day of Eid.

  5. Eid al-Ghadir

    The day of Ghadir.

  6. Winter camp

    Every January, for boys aged 10 to 17.

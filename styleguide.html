02 Type

Seven sizes, three families

Cormorant Garamond for headings, only at 24px and above, where its fine strokes hold up. Manrope for everything you read or tap. Amiri for Arabic. Nothing heavier than 600.

03 Structure

Numbered labels and gold hairlines, instead of boxes

    11 To approve

    Decisions for Mueez

    Reply with "approved", or with changes to any of the numbered items.

    1. Gold for text darkened from #8A6631 to #7D5D2B. The original fails AA on --paper-2 (4.2:1); the new value measures 4.87:1.

    2. Two support colours added: --ink-muted for secondary text and --mourning-muted for secondary text in Muharram mode.

    3. Crimson is never used for text (1.9:1 on mourning). It is used only for rules, the Live badge and the primary button fill.

    4. Near-square corners (2px) throughout, including chips: paper and stone, not app bubbles.

    5. Cormorant only at 24px and above. Smaller labels, meta and buttons use Manrope.

    6. Years are not counted up (1992 and 2018 simply appear). Only quantities count: 10 nights and 6 programs.

    7. Pinned scroll only on screens 768px and wider. Phones get a plain list with counters.

    8. Errors on light pages use crimson, always with an icon and a written message.
